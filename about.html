About Us

Who we are

Aadim Foundation is dedicated to the dignified, self-reliant and rights-based development of tribal and underserved rural communities across Maharashtra.

Our work rests on two pillars. We protect forests and build resilience to a changing climate, and we create livelihoods that keep families rooted to their land, so communities can shape their own future.

Forest protection & climate resilienceRights, stewardship and water
LivelihoodsFarming, enterprise and skills
How we work

With the community, never for it

Our approach is simple, and it is the same in every village.

Listen

Start with the Gram Sabha

We work only with the free, prior and informed consent of the community. The Gram Sabha decides; we facilitate.

Secure

Rights before projects

We help families claim and record their rights under the Forest Rights Act, because secure rights are the base for every other gain.

Build

Capacity that stays

Livelihoods, water and governance skills are built within the community, so the work continues without us.

Where we work. Rooted in Raigad, working across Maharashtra. Our office is at Titwala, Kalyan taluka, Thane district.
