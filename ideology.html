Ideology

What we believe

Rights, dignity and self-reliance, grounded in the values of the Constitution of India.

Vision & Mission

Where we are going, and how

Our vision

A Maharashtra where every tribal and rural community lives with dignity, holds secure rights over its land, forests and water, governs itself through its own gram sabha, and protects the forests that sustain all life.

असा महाराष्ट्र जिथे प्रत्येक आदिवासी व ग्रामीण समुदाय सन्मानाने जगतो, आपली जमीन, जंगल व पाणी यांवर त्याचे सुरक्षित हक्क आहेत, तो ग्रामसभेमार्फत स्वतःचा कारभार करतो आणि सर्व जीवसृष्टीला आधार देणाऱ्या जंगलाचे रक्षण करतो.

Our mission

Aadim Foundation works with tribal and underserved rural communities to secure their constitutional and legal rights, protect forests, build climate resilience and create dignified, self-reliant livelihoods, through community-led local governance.

आदिम फाउंडेशन आदिवासी व वंचित ग्रामीण समुदायांसोबत त्यांचे संवैधानिक व कायदेशीर हक्क मिळवून देण्यासाठी, जंगलांचे रक्षण करण्यासाठी, हवामान-सक्षमता वाढवण्यासाठी आणि सन्मानपूर्ण, स्वावलंबी उपजीविका उभारण्यासाठी, लोकाधारित स्थानिक स्वशासनाच्या माध्यमातून कार्य करते.

Constitutional foundation

Our ideology is the Constitution

The Preamble promises justice, liberty, equality and fraternity assuring the dignity of the individual. We exist to make that promise real for communities whose rights over land, forests and water are too often recognised on paper but not in practice.

Preamble

Justice, liberty, equality, fraternity

Social, economic and political justice, and the dignity of every individual, are the base of all our work.

Articles 14, 15 & 21

Equality and a life with dignity

No discrimination, equal protection of the law, and the right to live with dignity and livelihood.

Articles 39(b) & 46

Resources for the common good

Control of material resources to serve the common good, and special care for the interests of Scheduled Tribes.

Articles 48A & 51A(g)

Forests and the environment

The State’s duty to safeguard forests and wildlife, and every citizen’s duty to protect the natural environment.

Part IX (Art. 243) & PESA 1996

Self-government

Panchayats and the Gram Sabha as institutions of self-government, strengthened in Scheduled Areas by PESA.

Article 244 & Fifth Schedule

Scheduled Areas and the Forest Rights Act 2006

Protection of tribal land and administration, and recognition of individual and community forest rights.

Values in practice

Our values

These commitments guide every partnership and every conversation with the communities we serve.

01

Dignity & rights

We centre the dignity and legal rights of tribal and rural communities, especially under the Forest Rights Act and constitutional safeguards. We help people claim what the law already recognises as theirs.

02

Consent first

We work only with the free, prior and informed consent of the communities concerned. Gram Sabhas decide; we facilitate.

03

Self-reliance

We build lasting capacity in livelihoods, water and governance, rather than creating dependence.

04

Ecological stewardship

Forest, land and water are held in trust for present and future generations, and protected through community-based conservation.

05

Transparency

Open processes, open accounts and regular community feedback keep our work honest and responsive.

06

Equity & inclusion

Women, PVTGs, landless families and historically excluded groups have a voice at every stage.

We protect forests and build livelihoods so that tribal and rural communities can live with dignity, rights and self-reliance.
